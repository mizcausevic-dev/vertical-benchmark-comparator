# Origin

Vertical Benchmark Comparator exists because executive teams often talk about portfolio strength before they know which verticals truly deserve more investment, which ones are carrying the board story, and which ones still lack enough proof to justify their place. Kinetic Gain needs one reusable benchmark surface that compares flagship, watch, and underpowered lanes side by side so leaders can decide where to invest, where to hold, and what story to tell the board or investors.
